# USA Regional Sales Analysis

## Project Purpose

Analyze Acme Co.'s U.S. sales data from 2014--2018 to understand how
products, sales channels, customers, and regions contribute to revenue
and profit. The analysis explores sales trends, seasonality, unusual
transactions, and performance against the available 2017 budgets to
support data-driven business decisions.

## Project Objectives

-   Identify products, channels, customers, states, and regions that
    contribute most to revenue and profit.
-   Explore monthly and yearly sales patterns, including seasonal
    changes and unusual revenue movements.
-   Examine outliers in revenue and unit price, and understand
    relationships among quantity, price, cost, revenue, and profit.
-   Compare customer revenue with profit margins to identify customer
    segments and potential pricing or discount risks.
-   Present findings through visual analysis and support the development
    of an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

-   **Python:** Pandas, NumPy
-   **Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
-   **Notebook:** Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab
-   **Dashboard:** Power BI

## Project Workflow

1.  Load sales, customer, product, region, state-region, and budget
    data.
2.  Inspect data structure, data types, missing values, and duplicate
    rows.
3.  Merge related tables and standardize column names.
4.  Create analytical fields such as total cost, profit, profit margin,
    and order month.
5.  Perform exploratory data analysis using charts and grouped
    summaries.
6.  Summarize business insights and recommendations for sales planning.

## Key Areas of Analysis

-   Monthly sales trends and seasonality
-   Product revenue and profit-margin performance
-   Sales contribution by channel
-   State and U.S. regional performance
-   Customer revenue comparisons and segmentation
-   Average order value and transaction outliers
-   Correlations among quantity, unit price, revenue, cost, and profit

## Key Findings

-   Wholesale contributes the largest share of sales, followed by
    Distributor and Export channels.
-   California is the leading state by revenue and order count in the
    analysis.
-   Products 25 and 26 are among the top revenue contributors.
-   Export has the highest average profit margin among the three
    channels in the notebook analysis.
-   Revenue and unit price show strong positive relationships with
    profit, while quantity has a comparatively weaker relationship with
    the financial metrics.
-   A notable revenue dip in early 2017 warrants further investigation.

## Business Recommendations

-   Review unusual transactions and outliers before using averages for
    decisions.
-   Evaluate pricing, costs, and margins for lower-performing products.
-   Explore opportunities to grow Export channel sales while maintaining
    profitability.
-   Use monthly trends to plan promotions and investigate unexpected
    sales dips.
-   Use the analysis to inform an interactive Power BI dashboard for
    monitoring regional, product, channel, and customer performance.
